# Resume Writing Guidelines
- Put contact information and a concise professional summary near the top.
- Describe experience with measurable outcomes when the evidence supports them.
- Tailor relevant skills to the target role without inventing skills.
- Keep education and experience easy to scan.
- Use consistent dates, headings and formatting.
- Review spelling, grammar and file readability before submission.
- Certifications and courses should be listed accurately with their names and dates when known.
